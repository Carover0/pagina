Tu privacidad importa

When you submit the form to add your business to the map, only what you decide is published.

📍 About location

You don't have to share your exact address. You can use a generic point: the center of your city, a nearby square, or any approximate reference. The map will show that point and your business will still appear in the list.

💬 About contact details

They aren't required either. You choose what to show. You can use a public website, a Telegram channel, a Nostr handle, or leave the fields empty. Your business will still be listed.

🚫 What I DON'T do

I don't publish your IP or technical data. I don't sell or share your information. I don't use the data for anything other than this map.

👤 About this project

This is a personal project for the Firo community. If at any point you want your business edited or removed from the map, message me on Telegram at @cvg_1995 and we'll sort it out.

Everything you submit is reviewed manually before being published.
